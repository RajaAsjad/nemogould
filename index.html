About

Chairman of the Hoard

Self-described “Chairman of the Hoard,” Nemo Gould is a master accumulator of materials and building techniques alike. His Oakland studio is a veritable museum of old objects and technology, blended into a signature style of kinetic sculpture.

His work pushes the limits of found-object art and invites viewers to experience sculpture through interaction. Gould received his BFA from the Kansas City Art Institute (1998) and his MFA in sculpture from U.C. Berkeley (2000). His pieces appear in museums and collections throughout the U.S. and abroad.

Process

From scrap pile to kinetic life

Equal time spent hunting “raw” materials and coaxing them into new machines.

01

Salvage

Scrap yards, second-hand stores, and dumpsters — the studio begins in the field.

02

Sort & restore

Clean, strip, mend, and organize. If you don’t know what’s under the pile, it’s just garbage.

03

Fabricate

TIG welding, machining, electronics, and woodwork — no casting, only recontextualized parts.

04

Animate

Mechanics and wiring give each figure motion, light, and a personality of its own.
